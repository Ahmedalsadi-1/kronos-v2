The product is the environment. Wide frame, macro detail, then a return to connected context.
Retain the actual dark interface and mountain wallpaper. Silver surrounds are a resting stage, never the majority of every shot.
Titles use 600–700 weight at 84–140 px. Every title has a stable reading interval.
Camera travel changes magnification and focal point. Controlled 3–6px shake on two heavy landings.
Static captures are filmed as product stills. Only genuine captured state transitions can be cut together as UI activity.
All five required surfaces appear. Product development status remains explicit in closing copy.
