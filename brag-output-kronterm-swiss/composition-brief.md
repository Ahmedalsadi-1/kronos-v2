## Product / source
KronTerm is the AI-native workspace for builders. Terminal, browser, files, and AI agents share a persistent workspace. KronosCode is the engine; KronosChamber is its chat/control surface.
Source: website/src/components/KrontermAgentHero.tsx and KrontermCapabilitiesBento.tsx; repository product knowledge; website/public/assets/product/kronterm-cat-mark.png.
Concept UI illustrates actual surface names and website claims with fictional commands and diff data. No raw desktop screenshots, personal information, or Labs features.
Palette: Swiss cobalt/ivory/black retained by creative direction; actual KronTerm mascot used in product and brand shots.
