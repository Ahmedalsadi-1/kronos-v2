# The Chicago file

KronOS puts chat, devices and a sandbox terminal in one persistent workspace. A city research session leaves a trail from the article someone reads through the VM shortlist to the chat that started it. The camera follows that trail backwards.

## Storyboard

- 0–6.34 s. Open on a Chicago Riverwalk article scrolling on an iPhone. A river photograph from that same article becomes a printed travel spread. The destination earns the first six seconds.
- 6.34–12.65 s. Turn the page to the working notes. Three place names assemble alongside the guest terminal as it checks the Chicago shortlist. List entries appear in time with the score.
- 12.65–18.44 s. Trace the search to its source. The actual composer types “Find the best restaurants in Chicago.” Keep the finished question on screen.
- 18.44–20.50 s. Finish on the photographed KronOS wordmark.

## Direction

A polished city field guide, read from back to front. The hook is a destination, the payoff is its origin. Cream, copper and ink with Georgia editorial type. The phone and terminal are real recordings. Landscape 1920 × 1080, 20.5 seconds. This is an editorial reconstruction using the captured screens from the Chicago demo.
