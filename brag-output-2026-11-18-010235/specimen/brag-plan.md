# The working page
A print specimen for the KronOS canvas. Its three color studies come directly from the design in the recording. The viewer meets one page at three scales.
0–3.55: three real swatches introduce the page. 3.55–8.27: turn the sheet and watch a selected section move. 8.27–15.75: the canvas recording opens out from a frame to the whole spread. 15.75–19: the three inks close the spread, then the wordmark.
Landscape, polished editorial tone. Original tuned-mallet score, with pitched accents on the covers and page turns.
