# Split Decision

KronOS is a desktop workspace where two live tabs can share a pane. A hover preview can answer which tab you need before you open it. The entire film follows that one decision.

The hook is a giant split word, cut precisely at the same seam that will later separate the real workspace. Vermilion ink, graphite, cream type and hard rectangular masks tie the graphic to the software. The visible footage is the captured tab strip, successive hover cards and recorded two-pane transition. This works as an eighteen-second graphic-design short.

## Storyboard

- 0.00–2.65. SPLIT opens at its center. The top caption gives the word a second reading as a split decision. A narrow seam crosses the letterforms.
- 2.65–6.86. The two sides move aside to expose the first real hover card. The left rail reads “Before you open it.” A tiny magenta cursor takes the eye to the live preview.
- 6.86–10.01. Exchange the visual weight of the two columns. A closer cut shows the next preview entering under the pointer, finishing on the split action.
- 10.01–15.28. Both graphic columns give way to the real plane of the workspace. Two windows form side by side. A red rule picks up the window divider.
- 15.28–18.00. The rule sweeps the picture away. KronOS wordmark holds alone.

Music cue guidance uses bundled Business Moves Vol. 9 at approximately 115 BPM. The 2.65, 6.86, 10.01 and 15.28 second beats cue the four cuts. The sound mix uses three dry motion accents, then a short tail. The primary action comes from the native recording, with no spoken narration.
