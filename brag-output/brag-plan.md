# KronTerm — brag plan

**Tone:** default → polished launch energy. Motion-graphics heavy, dark UI, KronTerm green (#58C142 / #418D31 / #1F4D22), Inter + JetBrains Mono.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 22s. Music: happy-beats vol-10 (~110 BPM), cuts on its beat grid.

## Answers
- **What:** A macOS workspace that keeps terminal, browser, files and AI agents side by side in one persistent surface.
- **For:** Developers working with coding agents who are tired of re-building context across separate apps.
- **Different:** The agent (KronosCode) works on the same surfaces you see, and its commands, approvals and evidence stay visible. Four presentations of the same blocks: widgets, tabs, canvas, OS.
- **Best claim:** "Keep your terminal, browser, files, and AI in one persistent workspace."
- **Visual hook:** Four scattered app windows collapse into one workspace.
- **Real UI:** homepage screenshot (tiled workspace), sandbox screenshot, real copy ("@ for files/agents; / for commands and skills", "1 file changed +7 Review", website hero "Build and validate in the same field of view.").
- **Share line:** Terminal, browser, files and your agent, in one workspace you can actually see.
