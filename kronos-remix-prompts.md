# KronOS camera prompts

**Precision**  
Open tight on the caret. Track it laterally as the query appears. Hold long enough to read, then widen in a single move until the tab strip and its live preview are both legible. [Runway camera examples](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/47313504791059-Camera-Terms-Prompts-Examples)

**Graphite**  
Start tight on the illuminated composer against graphite. Ease backward until the entire workspace is revealed. Push through the sandbox pane as its desktop takes over the frame, settling before the browser action. [Runway pullback examples](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/47313504791059-Camera-Terms-Prompts-Examples)

**Spatial gallery**  
Begin close on the prompt. Glide back and up to reveal the recordings arranged in space. Travel sideways with the tab cursor, then settle squarely on the selected VM so its action reads. [Google Cloud camera examples](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/ultimate-prompting-guide-for-veo-3-1)
