# 14 - Canvas wheel interaction review

Read-only sample: `frontend/app/tab/workspace-canvas.tsx`; nearest guidance: `frontend/app/tab/AGENTS.md`. No builds or product edits. Findings are source observations; runtime effects below remain hypotheses.

## P2 - Normalize wheel input before camera math

**Observed:** `/Users/albsheralsadi/kronterm/frontend/app/tab/workspace-canvas.tsx:1996` applies raw `deltaY` to exponential zoom; line 2002 applies raw deltas to pan. Neither branch checks `deltaMode`; Shift always remaps vertical movement to horizontal. Existing nested widget/card handlers already stop wheel propagation (lines 561, 702, 741), so this review does not claim all widget scrolling is intercepted.

**Hypothesis:** Line-mode mouse events produce disproportionately small camera movement, and unconditional Shift remapping can disagree with platform-provided horizontal deltas. Electron device behavior needs reproduction before classifying this as a user-visible bug.

**Recommendation:** Extract a pure wheel-to-camera intent helper that converts pixel/line/page units and handles Shift according to observed platform behavior, preserving cursor-anchored zoom and clamp limits.

**Acceptance:** Equivalent pixel/line/page inputs yield equivalent pan distances; Ctrl/Command zoom preserves the world point under the cursor; horizontal trackpad and Shift+mouse scrolling work on supported macOS/Windows devices; nested terminal/card scrolling remains local.

**Verified pattern:** xyflow's [pan/zoom event handlers](https://github.com/xyflow/xyflow/blob/main/packages/system/src/xypanzoom/eventhandler.ts) explicitly normalize line-mode deltas and apply Shift remapping only outside macOS. Its [filter](https://github.com/xyflow/xyflow/blob/main/packages/system/src/xypanzoom/filter.ts) provides explicit nested-wheel exclusions. Transfer the policy/helper boundary rather than replacing KronTerm's persistent block ownership with graph nodes.

## P2 - Make wheel input interrupt camera momentum

**Observed:** `/Users/albsheralsadi/kronterm/frontend/app/tab/workspace-canvas.tsx:1283` continues frame-based camera updates until velocity decays. The wheel handler at line 1986 updates the same camera without calling `cancelCameraMomentum`; cancellation already exists at line 1257.

**Hypothesis:** A wheel gesture immediately after a fling can combine with residual momentum, moving the zoom anchor or producing unexpected drift.

**Recommendation:** Cancel momentum when accepting wheel pan/zoom input, using one camera interaction lifecycle. Keep expanded-widget and nested scroll exclusions ahead of that cancellation.

**Acceptance:** Fling then wheel-pan or wheel-zoom stops the previous momentum immediately; the new gesture controls the camera exclusively; persistence stores the final camera; reduced-motion behavior stays unchanged. Verify with deterministic animation-frame timing plus one trackpad interaction.
